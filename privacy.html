Privacy Policy

No Contact · Last updated 31 August 2026

No Contact is built to be private. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The only thing that ever leaves the app is a link you can choose to tap — see Links to partners.

What we store

Your no-contact start date and your progress are stored only on your device (and, for the widget, in a private app group on your device). This data never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or any server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a notification server and cannot see whether you receive them.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or the lifetime option, the transaction is processed by Apple, and the paywall and purchase status are managed through Superwall, our paywall provider. They process only the information needed to show the paywall and validate your purchase (such as an anonymous purchase identifier and subscription status). We never receive your payment details.

Links to partners

The “Right now” screen ends with a link to Online-Therapy.com, an independent online therapy service. It is an advertisement: if you subscribe after following that link, we earn a commission. We are not affiliated with them beyond that, and they send us no information about you.

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap it. Tapping it opens Safari, not a browser inside the app. From then on it is an ordinary web visit: the destination — and the click-tracking service that credits the referral (go.online-therapy.com) — will see your IP address, your browser’s user agent and the time of the visit, and may set a cookie in Safari so the referral can be attributed. That exchange is between you and them, governed by their privacy policy. The app does not send it, and never learns whether you subscribed or what you discuss with anyone.

Analytics & tracking

We do not use analytics SDKs, third-party ad networks, or cross-app tracking, and the app does not request tracking permission. The only advertisement in the app is the partner link described above: it is a plain link, it loads no ad-network code, and it does nothing at all until you tap it.

Children

No Contact is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.
