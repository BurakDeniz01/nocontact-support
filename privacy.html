Privacy Policy

No Contact · Last updated 31 May 2026

No Contact is built to be private. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

What we store

Your no-contact start date and your progress are stored only on your device (and, for the widget, in a private app group on your device). This data never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or any server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a notification server and cannot see whether you receive them.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or the lifetime option, the transaction is processed by Apple, and purchase status is managed through RevenueCat, our in-app purchase provider. They process only the information needed to validate your purchase (such as an anonymous purchase identifier and receipt). We never receive your payment details.

Analytics & tracking

We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking, and the app does not request tracking permission.

Children

No Contact is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.
