Privacy Policy

No Contact · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy covers the No Contact app for iOS and Android and, where available, its widget.

No Contact is built to be private. Your content — the reasons you type, journal entries, unsent letters and voice notes — stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. We do not sell or share your personal data. Our paywall and purchase provider receives limited technical and purchase information and, on iPhone, Apple Ads attribution information, none of it linked to your identity, and your app store (Apple or Google) handles purchases — see Purchases and Apple Ads attribution. Apart from that, the only thing that leaves the app is a link you can choose to tap — see Links to partners.

What we store

Your no-contact start date and your progress are stored only on your device. On iPhone, the widget reads them from a private app group on your device; on Android, the widget reads them from the app’s own local data on your device. The same goes for everything you create in the app: the reasons you type during setup, journal entries, unsent letters and voice notes are stored only on your device. None of it is sent to us or any server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, every one of them — daily notes, milestone notes, a short note back after a letter you keep, and 30-day look-backs at your journal — is written and scheduled locally on your device. Milestone notes can quote the reasons you typed during setup; those reasons stay on your phone, and you can turn quoting off in Settings. Nothing is sent to a server: we do not run a notification server and cannot see whether you receive them.

On-device AI

On-device AI is available on iPhone only; the Android app does not include any AI features. On iPhone, private reflections on your letters and “Talk it through” use Apple’s on-device model, available on iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on. What you write there is processed on your iPhone only. Conversations are not saved: they disappear when you close the screen. Nothing you write is sent to us, to Apple’s servers or to any AI provider, and there is no account involved.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap record to add a voice note to your journal. Recordings are stored on your device and are never uploaded.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or the lifetime option, the transaction is processed by your app store: Apple (App Store) on iPhone, or Google (Google Play Billing) on Android. On both platforms, the paywall and purchase status are managed through Superwall, our paywall provider. Superwall is set up when the app opens, so for every user it receives an anonymous device identifier and an app-assigned ID, and paywall interaction events (such as a paywall being shown or closed), and, if you buy, your purchase history and subscription status. If a short optional question appears after a purchase, the choice you tap (from fixed options; no typing) is sent to Superwall with the same anonymous identifier. Superwall also derives an approximate location from your IP address and receives device diagnostic data (crash and performance data). This data, and the subscription information described in the next paragraph, is used only for app functionality (including choosing the message described there) and analytics; it is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking. None of your content (reasons, journal, letters, voice notes) is ever sent. We never receive your payment details.

On iPhone, when you open your No Contact subscription in your Apple Account settings, Apple sends Superwall your subscription’s transaction ID, product and device language so it can choose a short message to show there. The message is chosen only by product and language; none of your content is involved.

Superwall processes the purchase and paywall data described in this section only on our behalf and on our instructions, under its Data Processing Addendum, and gives it the same or equal protection as this policy (Superwall Privacy Policy, Superwall Data Processing Addendum). Superwall keeps this data for as long as No Contact uses its service. To ask for it to be deleted, email z.burakdeniz@gmail.com; we pass the request to Superwall, which helps us carry it out under that addendum.

On Android, the app also uses these Google Play components, which Google operates under Google’s privacy policy:

  • Google Play Billing processes purchases. Google may process purchase and billing diagnostic data as part of this.
  • Google Play Install Referrer tells the app which Play Store listing or campaign it was installed from.
  • Google Play In-App Review may show Google’s rating prompt; any rating or review you leave goes to Google, not to us.
  • Google Play services provides the system services these components rely on.

The Android app has no advertising ID, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

Apple Ads attribution

On iPhone, the Superwall library in the app gets an attribution token on your device from Apple’s AdServices framework and sends it to Superwall. With it, Superwall learns whether the install came from one of our ads on Apple Ads and, if so, details such as the campaign, ad group and keyword, and the country or region. We use this only to measure how our own ads perform. It is not linked to your identity, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites or to show you ads. Neither the token nor what Superwall learns from it contains any of your content (reasons, journal entries, unsent letters, voice notes or anything you write in “Talk it through”). In App Store privacy terms this is advertising data not linked to your identity. The Android app does not use Apple Ads attribution.

Links to partners

The “Right now” screen ends with a link to Online-Therapy.com, an independent online therapy service. It is an advertisement: if you subscribe after following that link, we earn a commission. We are not affiliated with them beyond that, and they send us no information about you.

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap it. Tapping it opens your browser (Safari on iPhone, your default browser on Android), not a browser inside the app. From then on it is an ordinary web visit: the destination — and the click-tracking service that credits the referral (go.online-therapy.com) — will see your IP address, your browser’s user agent and the time of the visit, and may set a cookie in your browser so the referral can be attributed. That exchange is between you and them, governed by their privacy policy. The app does not send it, and never learns whether you subscribed or what you discuss with anyone.

Helpline directory

Support screens in the app — and Settings — include a link to findahelpline.com, an independent directory of free helplines by country. It is not an advertisement and not a partner: we earn nothing from it and it sends us no information about you. Like the link above, nothing is sent unless you tap it, and tapping opens your browser. From then on it is an ordinary web visit governed by that site’s own privacy policy. The app never learns that you tapped it.

Analytics & tracking

We do not add analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own, and we do not use third-party ad networks or cross-app tracking; the app does not request tracking permission. The only usage and diagnostic data that leaves the app is the paywall interaction events, the optional post-purchase answer, approximate location and diagnostic data described under Purchases and, on iPhone, the Apple Ads attribution described under Apple Ads attribution, all of which are not linked to your identity, and, on Android, the data Google’s Play components handle as described there. The only advertisement in the app is the partner link described above: it is a plain link, it loads no ad-network code, and it does nothing at all until you tap it.

Children

No Contact is for people aged 18 and over (see the Terms of Use). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
