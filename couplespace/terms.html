Shared content and keepsakes

Everything you add to a shared space is shared with your partner. Either of you can save copies of it at any time. When a shared space ends, because one of you leaves or deletes it, each of you keeps a read-only copy of the shared content in a keepsake box on your own device, provided you both use an up-to-date version of the app. Deleting the space removes it from iCloud, but it does not remove copies your partner already holds. We have no access to those copies and cannot delete them for you. Only add content you are comfortable with your partner keeping.

When either of you deletes a memory, drawing or special day, it moves to Recently deleted in Settings for 30 days. Either of you can restore it during that time. After 30 days it is deleted permanently.

Ortak alana eklediğiniz her şey partnerinizle paylaşılır. İkiniz de bu içeriğin kopyasını istediğiniz an kaydedebilirsiniz. Ortak alan sona erdiğinde, yani biriniz ayrıldığında ya da alanı sildiğinde, her biriniz ortak içeriğin salt okunur bir kopyasını kendi cihazınızdaki hatıra kutusunda saklarsınız (ikinizin de uygulamanın güncel sürümünü kullanması koşuluyla).
