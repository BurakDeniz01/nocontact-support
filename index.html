How the counter works

Set the day your no-contact began during onboarding. The app counts whole calendar days from that date, so it stays correct across time zones and daylight-saving changes.

What does "I reached out" do?

If you slip and contact them, tap I reached out to start a new stretch from today. Your furthest day reached is kept — your progress is never erased.

Milestones

Tap the trail to see supportive notes for Day 7, 14, 30, 60, and 90. These are encouragement, not medical or clinical advice.

Subscription & billing

No Contact offers an optional subscription (monthly or yearly) and a one-time lifetime option. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a previous purchase, use Restore on the paywall.

Privacy

Your streak stays on your device. We don't collect your personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
